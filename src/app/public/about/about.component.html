About Gym For Healthy 🦍

Welcome to Gym For Healthy - your all-in-one platform designed to elevate the fitness experience for
both members and trainers.

Our mission is simple: *Stronger. Healthier. Happier.*

This system was built to:

  • Help gym members track their classes, body measurements, and progress.
  • Enable trainers to manage their lessons and support their clients effectively.
  • Allow administrators to oversee the gym's operations efficiently.

Key Features:

  • - Weekly lesson schedules and class enrollments
  • - Package tracking (Full & Weekly)
  • - Trainer-to-member communication

Gym For Healthy was designed and developed by Görkem Karagöz as part of a backend development internship project with TÜRKTRUST.
